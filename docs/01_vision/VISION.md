# Vision: warehouse-microservice

status: approved
completeness_level: complete

## One-Sentence Vision
Maintain authoritative stock and inventory operations for commerce consumers.

## Problem Statement
The ecosystem needs this responsibility to have a clear, durable, and safe ownership boundary.

## Target Users
Orders, marketplace integrations, warehouse operators, and commerce administrators.

## Core User Need
Consumers need the documented capability without ambiguity about ownership or operational safety.

## Key Outcomes
Inventory and stock management for the e-commerce ecosystem.

## Non-Goals
The vision does not expand this repository into unrelated ecosystem domains.

## Success Criteria
The documented boundary remains usable and future work preserves its approved constraints.
